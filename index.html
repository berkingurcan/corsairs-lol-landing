The loop

Capture. Raise. Get taken. Take it back.

Five moves, and none of them need explaining twice.

  1. 01

    Capture

    An unclaimed country opens at 0.05 SOL. Two taps, signed in your wallet.

  2. 02

    Raise your banner

    Write a title, a tagline, a link and a colour onto the territory. It is embedded on chain as a Memo instruction.

  3. 03

    Get taken

    Anyone can take the country from you by paying +20% of the last price. That is the whole defence: there isn't one.

  4. 04

    Get settled

    The takeover is designed to pay the displaced captain their full principal plus 70% of the increase, in the same transaction.

  5. 05

    Reclaim

    Open the map and take it back whenever you want. Nothing pings your phone — you have to come looking.

The banner

A country is a slot. The banner is what you put in it.

Owning a territory is not a bag and not a badge. It is 302 characters and a colour, standing on a world map until somebody pays more than you did.

The numbers

What a takeover is designed to move.

Every price step is +20%. Of that increase, 70% is routed to the captain who just lost the country and 30% to the Harbor Treasury. The displaced captain gets their principal back on top.

Read this before you read the table. These are the configured parameters and a worked example of the price step — not a promised return, and not a record of anything that has happened. The settlement path is written into the code and has not yet been proven by a real takeover on mainnet. When one settles, the receipt goes up with a signature you can open in an explorer.

Price authority

The app does not decide what a country costs.

The server prices every purchase

It locks the territory row, prices it in integer lamports, and builds the transaction your wallet signs. There is exactly one transfer constructor in the codebase and it is server-side. A patched build cannot pay one lamport for a country.

One buyer at a time

A partial unique index allows exactly one open intent per country. The second simultaneous buyer gets a 409 before their wallet ever opens — so there is no second payment and nothing to refund.

Settlement is checked on chain

The confirmed transaction is read back: who signed it, and what each account actually gained. Ownership changes only after that check passes, under a version compare-and-set.

Ownership is a row in our database. The chain is the payment rail and the banner ledger; the server is the ownership ledger. We say it that way on purpose. There is no custom on-chain program and no audit — a capture is a SOL transfer plus a memo.

What it is not

No token. No points. No airdrop. Nothing to farm.

The game is played in SOL. There is no corsairs.lol token and there will not be one. That is a permanent commitment, not a phase.

  • Not a token playThere is nothing to buy but a country.
  • Not a memecoinWhat you own is a country and a banner, not a bag.
  • Not an airdrop farmNo points, no seasons, no snapshot.
  • Not fully on-chainOwnership is a server row. We do not call it trustless.
  • Not auditedNo custom program exists to audit.
  • Not on iPhoneAndroid and Seeker only.
